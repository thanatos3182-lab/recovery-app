# Chrysalis

A free, private Android app for people working on their recovery. Everything stays on your phone: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and the app doesn't have internet access.

## What's inside

- **Right now:** Craving SOS, My Plan, support contacts, one-tap 988
- **Daily:** check-ins, pattern insights, milestones and savings, appointments with reminders, meetings, daily structure (plan your day and cover your hardest hours), counselor summary you can share
- **Next steps:** local jobs, job tracker, housing search, housing tracker
- **Learn:** coping skills with how and why they work, plus neuroscience lessons on addiction, habits, stress, sleep, and how the brain changes
- **Find a sponsor:** how it works and where to meet people
- **Daily letter:** a thought, a brain fact, and a small challenge each day

## Install (Android 7.0 or newer)

1. On your phone, open the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and tap the **.apk** file under Assets to download it.
2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser. That's a one-time switch.
3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns that the app is unrecognized, choose **Install anyway**. It's not in the Play Store.

iPhone isn't supported.

## Privacy

Your entries, check-ins, plan, and trackers are stored only on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes your data. Sharing a counselor summary happens only when you tap Share and pick where it goes.

## Please read

Chrysalis is a self-help tool. It's not medical care and doesn't replace a doctor, counselor, or treatment program. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text **988**.
